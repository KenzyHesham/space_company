# space_company
Interactive educational space adventure where NASA's iconic Moon and Mars hardware and rovers (Apollo 11 Eagle, Lunar Roving Vehicle, Spirit, and Opportunity) share their real mission stories to their new friends.

Created for the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge — "Abandoned, but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars,"** 
This project introduces school-age space enthusiasts to the historic hardware humanity left behind across the solar system.

When kids read about space exploration, they usually hear about active missions or get lost in dense technical specs. I wanted to spark their imagination by asking a simple question: *Ever feel lonely on Earth or wish you had an extraordinary friend? Millions of miles away on the Moon and Mars, brave NASA explorers are waiting to share their untold stories."*

**Space Company** turns dry mission archives into an interactive adventure where young explorers launch a virtual rocket from Earth to meet four NASA legends and hear their stories in the first person[cite: 42, 43]:

- **Apollo 11 Lunar Module ("Eagle")** — Still resting at Tranquility Base on the Moon since July 1969[cite: 37, 38].
- **Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV)** — Parked at Hadley–Apennine on the Moon since 1971[cite: 41, 42].
- **Opportunity (MER-B)** — Silent in Meridiani Planum, Mars, since the 2018 dust storm[cite: 41, 42].
- **Spirit (MER-A)** — Resting in Gusev Crater, Mars, since 2010[cite: 41, 42].

## Built for Young Explorers

- **Fly the Roadmap:** Launch from Earth to the Moon or Mars to unlock each explorer[cite: 42, 43].
- **Chat with the Hardware:** Experience bite-sized, first-person mission stories designed for school-age readers, complete with memory checks, expandable NASA data sheets, and an interactive 3D model to inspect spacecraft parts[cite: 37, 38].
- **Play Buddy Surface Missions:** Step onto the Moon and Mars in a kid-friendly 2D co-op game (with a dedicated Kid Explorer mode) to deploy real NASA science instruments, collect Moon rocks, and brush dust off Opportunity's solar panels[cite: 40, 41].
- **Earn Ranks & Customizations:** Collect points, badges, and new spacesuit or spacecraft finishes that carry over across every page[cite: 37, 38, 41, 43].
